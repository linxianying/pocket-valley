# 🌼 口袋山谷 · Pocket Valley

A cozy storybook watercolor farming, ranching, and cooking game playable directly in the browser — built as a single self-contained HTML5 Canvas app with Chinese / English support and local auto-save.

一款画风柔和的田园绘本风模拟经营小游戏，打开浏览器即可直接游玩，支持中/英文切换与本地自动存档。

## ✨ Features / 特色玩法

- **🏡 Home Farm / 田园农庄**: Till soil, plant **10 varieties of seeds** (Turnip, Wheat, Radish, Carrot, Tomato, Sweet Corn, Strawberry, Blueberry, Pumpkin, Starfruit), water daily, refill at the lotus pond, and automate watering with **Quality Sprinklers**.
- **🐮 Sunny Pasture / 阳光牧场**: Walk East through the garden gate to adopt and raise **Chickens, Ducks, Sheep, and Cows**, feed them hay, pet them for hearts, and collect fresh Eggs, Duck Eggs, Soft Wool, and Fresh Milk every morning.
- **🍳 Cottage Kitchen & 10 Recipes / 小屋厨房与菜谱烹饪**: Upgrade your cottage kitchen to cook 10 dishes (Garden Salad, Sunny Omelet, Honey Butter Toast, Creamy Carrot Stew, Tomato Corn Soup, Strawberry Shortcake, Blueberry Pie, Caramel Egg Pudding, Golden Pumpkin Soup, and Starlight Fruit Tart) using harvested crops, ranch produce, or bought pantry staples.
- **👗 Cute Cottagecore Protagonist / 可爱田园主角**: Twin braided pigtails, ribboned straw sunhat, and 4 selectable floral farm dresses.
- **🌐 Bilingual / 中英双语**: Instant `中文 / EN` toggle in the top bar.

## 🎮 Controls / 操作说明

- `WASD` / `Arrow Keys` (or click anywhere): Walk & interact
- `Left-Click` / `Space`: Till, water, plant, clear debris
- `E` / `Right-Click`: Harvest crops, pet animals, collect pasture drops, enter shops/cottage
- `1`–`9`, `0` / Mouse Wheel: Switch tools & seeds

## 🚀 Play Locally / 本地游玩

Just open `index.html` directly in any modern browser — no build step or server required!
